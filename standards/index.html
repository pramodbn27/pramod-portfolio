IEEE Standards leadership

IEEE P4211 & IEEE P4213

Serving as Working Group Chair for IEEE P4211 and IEEE P4213, leading international standards development initiatives focused on operational lifecycle management, governance, and end-to-end observability of production AI systems.

IEEE P4211Active PAR

Standard for Operational Lifecycle Management and Governance of Generative Artificial Intelligence Systems in Production Environments

Standards body
IEEE Standards Association (IEEE SA)
Role
Working Group Chair
Working group
GenAIOps-WG, Generative Artificial Intelligence Operations Working Group
Sponsor
C/AISC, Artificial Intelligence Standards Committee (IEEE Computer Society)
PAR approved
2026-09-25

Developing a unified operational framework for organizations deploying and operating GenAI systems in production, covering:

  • Deployment & release
  • Evaluation & validation
  • Runtime monitoring
  • Security operations
  • Operational safety controls
  • Incident management
  • Change management
  • Lifecycle governance

IEEE P4213Active PAR

Standard for Observability of Artificial Intelligence Systems

Standards body
IEEE Standards Association (IEEE SA)
Role
Working Group Chair
Working group
AISO-WG, Artificial Intelligence Systems Observability Working Group
Sponsor
C/AISC, Artificial Intelligence Standards Committee
PAR approved
2026-09-25

Developing a unified observability framework for instrumenting, monitoring, measuring and validating AI systems in production, encompassing:

  • AI models
  • Inference & serving
  • Agentic & multi-agent workflows
  • RAG pipelines
  • Telemetry
  • Performance
  • Reliability
  • Supporting infrastructure

Driving industry collaboration and consensus-based standardization to advance reliable, observable, safe, and governable AI systems at scale.
